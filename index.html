DIGITAL IMAGE MAKER

I am a hybrid... I am an image maker.

My approach to art emerges entirely from the unconscious. I never consciously think about what to do; it all flows naturally, as in automatic writing. It's a deep-seated need to express myself. For that, I employ various techniques: I use photography to help create an image, scan objects to transform them into compositions, and often overlay elements. Drawing heavily upon my experience as a painter, and treating post-processing as an essential tool, I view myself not merely as a photographer or painter, but as a hybrid image maker.
